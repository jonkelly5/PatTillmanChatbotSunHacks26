Pat's Run is an annual running event held in Tempe, Arizona in honor of Pat Tillman — namesake of ASU's The Pat Tillman Veterans Center. A former NFL player who left his professional football career to enlist in the United States Army after the September 11 attacks, Tillman was killed in Afghanistan in 2004 due to friendly fire.

Pat's Run has grown significantly since its inception and attracts thousands of participants each year, including military personnel, veterans, athletes, and community members, all coming together to honor Tillman's legacy and support a worthy cause.

The run consists of a 4.2-mile race, which symbolizes Tillman's jersey number when he played football for Arizona State University. The event serves as a fundraiser for the Pat Tillman Foundation, which provides scholarships to military veterans and their spouses.

What is the Pat's Run?
Pat’s Run is an annual 4.2-mile race held to honor Pat Tillman. The fundraising event supports scholarships for service members, veterans, and military spouses.

Why is Pat's Run 4.2 miles?
Pat’s Run is 4.2 miles long to honor Pat Tillman’s football jersey number 42. Pat wore this number when he played football at Arizona State University. The course ends at the 42-yard line in Sun Devil Stadium.

What is the race distance for Pat's Run?
Pat’s Run is 4.2 miles. The Kids Run is 0.42 miles. The 4.2-mile distance honors Pat Tillman’s football jersey number 42.

What is a Tillman Honor Run, and how is it different from Pat's Run?
Tillman Honor Runs are 4.2-mile community events hosted across the United States. Pat’s Run takes place in Tempe, Arizona, at Sun Devil Stadium. Honor Runs occur in your local city on different dates throughout April.
